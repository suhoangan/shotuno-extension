## Detailed description

```
Shotuno captures screenshots and lets you annotate them without leaving the tab.

Capture. Mark up. Stay in the tab.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Click the Shotuno icon in your Chrome toolbar
2. Choose Visible, Selected Area, Full Page, or Pin area
3. Annotate on the in-page canvas, then copy, download, or pin for later

CAPTURE MODES
• Visible Content — grab what’s on screen right now
• Selected Area — draw a region, then edit
• Full Page — scroll and stitch the entire page
• Pin area — save a region to the side panel and reuse it on any tab
• Open image — load a local image into the editor

ANNOTATION TOOLS
Arrows & lines, shapes, text & callouts, brush, highlighter, blur/pixelate (redact sensitive info), counters, magnifier, measure, crop, stickers, and on-device OCR text extraction.

LIBRARY
Open Pins & Downloads in the Chrome side panel. Drag saved shots onto web pages, or send an image to Gemini when you choose.

PRIVACY-FIRST
• Free forever. No account required.
• Screenshots and pins stay on your device (Chrome local storage)
• No crash reporting, analytics, or screenshot uploads to Shotuno servers

Support the project (optional tip): open Buy me a coffee from the popup or side panel.
```
